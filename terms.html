AI simulations, not people

Everything a twin says is generated by artificial intelligence. This matters, so we say it plainly:

  • A twin is a fictional simulation built from the material you provide. It is not the real person, does not speak for them, and its replies are predictions — not facts, not their actual views, and not messages from them.
  • Twins can be wrong, inconsistent, or unconvincing. Do not make important decisions based solely on what a twin says.
  • If you use aiwillmake to revisit conversations with someone who has passed away or is otherwise out of reach, know that the twin is a reflection assembled from your own memories and messages. It can be a space to say what was left unsaid — but it is not the person, and nothing it says comes from them.

If grief, a breakup, or a difficult relationship is weighing on you heavily, an AI tool is not a substitute for human support. Please consider talking to someone you trust or a mental-health professional.
